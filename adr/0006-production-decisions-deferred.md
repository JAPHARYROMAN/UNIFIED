# ADR 0006: Production Decisions Deferred Behind Neutral Interfaces

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-07-24
- **Owner:** Protocol Architecture Authority

## Decision

Production home chain, legal entities, jurisdictions, identity and payment
providers, bridge, oracle, custody, insurance, taxation, and deployment regions
remain undecided. The foundation must model them through versioned identifiers,
policy references, evidence, adapters, and explicit finality without selecting
vendors or embedding local defaults.

No public testnet work may begin until the decisions it consumes are accepted
ADRs with Security, Accounting/Economic Risk, and Release review as applicable.
